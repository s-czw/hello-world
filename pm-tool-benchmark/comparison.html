Program & project management · tool benchmark · July 2026

Nine PM platforms, scored for a Microsoft 365 team that runs programs

Benchmark of Jira, Monday.com, Asana, ClickUp, Wrike, Smartsheet, Microsoft Planner/Project, Airtable, and Zoho Projects for a 10–50 person team doing mixed operations work. Two hard requirements drove the scoring: a program/portfolio layer (multi-project roll-ups, cross-project Gantt and dependencies, portfolio dashboards) and deep Microsoft 365 / Teams integration. Pricing and features verified as of 20 July 2026.

The verdict

Based on the default weighting below, which puts half the total weight on the two must-haves. Drag the sliders in the next section to stress-test these picks against your own priorities.

Top pick

Asana

Advanced · $24.99/user/mo · 4.40 weighted

The only tool that satisfies both must-haves out of the box on a published tier: unlimited and nested Portfolios with cross-project Gantt, workload and Goals — plus the deepest third-party Microsoft stack (native Teams app, Outlook add-in, certified Power Automate connector, and a native Power BI connector).

Caveats: priciest published portfolio tier here ($625/mo at 25 seats); SAML SSO requires Enterprise; no built-in docs surface.

Runner-up

Smartsheet

Business · $19/member/mo · 4.20 weighted

Equal-deepest M365 integration — the only tool with official Microsoft-published Power Automate and Power BI connectors, plus Teams tabs with in-Teams approvals. Reports roll up thousands of sheets into portfolio Gantt dashboards, and external guests are free and unlimited.

Caveats: dependencies work within a single sheet only; full program governance (Control Center) is a custom-priced add-on; SSO is Enterprise-only.

Value pick

Zoho Projects

Enterprise · $9/user/mo · 3.70 weighted

A genuine portfolio layer — portfolio dashboard, global Gantt, and real inter-project dependencies — at $225/mo for 25 users, roughly a third of the other portfolio-capable tiers. Solid Teams tab app, Outlook add-in, and even an M365 Copilot app.

Caveats: busier UI with a 2–3 week onboarding curve; no Power Automate or Power BI connectors; lighter admin governance.

Method & sources

How this was researched. Three parallel research passes on 20 July 2026, each covering three tools, using vendor pricing/feature pages, vendor help-center documentation, Microsoft Learn/AppSource listings, G2 and Capterra, and 2025–2026 analyst comparisons (Gartner MQ for Adaptive Project Management & Reporting, Forbes Advisor, PCMag, TechRadar). Several vendor pricing pages block automated access, so figures were cross-checked across at least two independent 2026-dated sources; the top three tools’ prices were re-verified separately. Scores are 1–5 judgments against this team’s specific context (10–50 seats, mixed ops, M365 shop) — not absolute product quality.

Analyst signals. Gartner Leaders (2024–25): Monday.com (4 consecutive years), Asana, Atlassian. G2 positions Smartsheet as the pick for “large-scale project & portfolio planning”. PCMag Editors’ Choice: Zoho Projects and Asana. Forbes Advisor (Feb 2026) rates Monday.com “best for new teams” and ClickUp best value for most SMBs.

Key dated facts. Jira Work Management merged into Jira (2024). Microsoft retired Project for the web in Aug 2025; its successor is Planner “premium plans” with the new Portfolios feature; Project Online retires 30 Sep 2026. Wrike’s standalone Enterprise plan is end-of-sale, replaced by Pinnacle/Apex. Smartsheet moved to a member/guest subscription model (guests free and unlimited).

Full source list (per-tool URLs and access dates) lives in pm-tool-benchmark/research-notes.md in the repository alongside this page.
